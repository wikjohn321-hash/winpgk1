WinPGK referral: invite friends and share your link

WinPGK has a referral section so players can share their link and track their downline.

What the referral section offers

The platform advertises its referral area with the tagline "Share to earn more!" and four options:

  • Invite: send your invite to friends.
  • Copy Link: copy your personal link to share.
  • Downline: see the players you invited.
  • More Info: read the current referral terms.

The rates and conditions that apply to your account are on the platform's referral page, and those are the ones that count.

How to get started

  1. Register and log in.
  2. Open the referral section and copy your link.
  3. Share it with people you know.

Invite responsibly

Only invite adults aged 18 or over, and be honest that gambling carries risk and can lose money. Referral rewards depend on what other people do, so never treat them as guaranteed income. Fake or multiple accounts are not tolerated.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my referral link?

Open the referral section on the platform after you log in and use Copy Link.

How much do I earn from referrals?

The platform's More Info page shows the current rates and conditions. Check it there, because it is the final word.

Can I refer myself with a second account?

No. Multiple accounts are not allowed and can lead to forfeited credits.
