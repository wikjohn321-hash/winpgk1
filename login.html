WinPGK login: how to sign in to your account

Use the Login button to sign in with the details you chose when you registered.

How to log in

  1. Press Login on this page to open the WinPGK sign-in screen.
  2. Enter the username and password you created at registration.
  3. After you sign in you can deposit, claim promotions and open the game lobby.
Login to WinPGK

Two different logins

Your WinPGK account and your 918Kaya game account are separate. 918Kaya is a standalone app with its own login ID and password, issued to you through your WinPGK account. See the 918Kaya guide for details.

If you cannot log in

  • Check you are typing the password exactly, including capital letters.
  • Make sure you are on the real platform page and not a copy. Only use the button on this page or the link you were given.
  • Use the live chat on the platform if you still cannot get in.
  • Do not register a second account to get around a login problem. One player can only have one account.
