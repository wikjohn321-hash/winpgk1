WinPGK deposit: minimum K30 and how it works

WinPGK deposits are made by bank transfer in Kina. The minimum deposit is K30.

Quick facts

Minimum depositK30
MethodBank transfer (BSP Bank or Kina Bank). Cards are not accepted.
CurrencyKina (K)

Step by step

  1. Log in and open the Deposit screen.
  2. Enter your amount (K30 or more) and choose the bank.
  3. The screen shows the bank account to pay and a reference number. Use that reference as the payee reference in your transfer.
  4. Make the transfer in your own banking app or by SMS banking.
  5. Fill in the transfer method and time, upload your transfer slip, and press Submit.
Register on WinPGK

Slips and banking

Most players in PNG transfer by SMS banking. The bank sends a slip by SMS, and you take a screenshot of it and upload it. Kina Bank customers can also use WhatsApp banking.

The bank account to pay can change from time to time. Always copy the details from your own deposit screen before every deposit, and never use details from a screenshot or message someone sends you.

Before you submit

  • Your transfer must be from an account in your own name, the same as your register name.
  • Check the amount and reference number match exactly.
  • Only deposit money you can afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum deposit on WinPGK?

The minimum deposit is K30.

Can I deposit with a card?

No. WinPGK uses bank transfer, with BSP Bank and Kina Bank as the supported banks.

Where do I find the bank account to pay?

On your own deposit screen after you log in. The details can change, so check them every time.
