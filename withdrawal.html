WinPGK withdrawal: minimum K100, rules and steps

Withdrawals go to the bank account you registered with. The minimum withdrawal is K100.

Quick facts

Minimum withdrawalK100
Paid toThe bank account saved on your account
Rollover on a normal deposit1x (wager your deposit once before it can be withdrawn)

Step by step

  1. Make sure you have logged out of the game you were playing.
  2. Open the Withdraw screen on the platform.
  3. Check the bank details shown. They come from your registration and are not typed in again at withdrawal.
  4. Enter the withdrawal amount (K100 or more) and submit.
Register on WinPGK

Rules that catch players out

  • Rollover: a normal deposit needs 1x rollover. For example, deposit K50 and you need to wager K50 before that money is withdrawable.
  • Bank details: your register name must match the bank holder name. Bank account changes are only allowed with a deposit record.
  • One account: each player may have one account. If multiple accounts are found, accounts can be closed and credits forfeited.
  • Bonuses: free bonuses have their own withdrawal rules. See the free K28 terms.
Withdrawal limits can differ for a bonus balance. Check the limit shown on your own withdraw screen before you request.

Bank account changes are only allowed with a deposit record, so enter the correct bank details when you register.
