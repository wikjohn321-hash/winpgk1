WinPGK register: how to sign up in Papua New Guinea

Registering on WinPGK takes a few minutes. Here is what to prepare and the rules to know before you press Submit.

Step by step

  1. Press the Register button on this page to open the WinPGK registration form.
  2. Fill in the form with your own details. Your register name must be the same as your bank account holder name.
  3. Choose a password you do not use on other sites.
  4. Log in to your new account.
  5. If you want the new register bonus, open the promotion page and read the terms first. See the free K28 guide.

Before you register

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Have your own bank account ready. Bank account changes are only allowed with a deposit record, so enter your details correctly the first time.
  • Use only one account per player. Fake or multiple accounts are not tolerated and credits can be forfeited.
  • Similar IP addresses detected on different accounts can also cause credits to be forfeited.

After you register

Next you can read the deposit guide (minimum K30) or the withdrawal guide (minimum K100). To play 918Kaya, see the 918Kaya guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to register?

Registering is the first step. The platform shows any requirements before you deposit. The new register bonus of K28 is advertised as no deposit required.

Why must my name match my bank holder name?

WinPGK's terms say the register name must be the same as the bank holder. Use your real name exactly as it appears on your bank account.

Can I have more than one account?

No. The terms allow one player one account. Multiple accounts can lead to forfeited credits.
